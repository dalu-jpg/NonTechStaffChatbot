SECTION 1: ACCOUNT ACCESS & IDENTITY MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 Domain Account Lockout Policy
- Accounts lock automatically after 5 consecutive failed login attempts.
- Automatic lockout duration is 15 minutes.
- Immediate Unlock: Staff can self-service unlock or reset passwords via the official portal at https://auth.senexaid.org/reset using registered Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).

1.2 Password Requirements & Reset Guidelines
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include: 1 uppercase letter, 1 lowercase letter, 1 number, and 1 special character (!@#$%^&*).
- Passwords expire every 90 days.
- If locked out completely without access to MFA, email support@senexaid.org with your Staff ID and Supervisor approval.

1.3 Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Setup
- All staff must register Microsoft Authenticator or Google Authenticator.
- SMS-based OTP is restricted to authorized field teams operating in low-bandwidth regions.
- To re-bind MFA to a new phone, log into https://auth.senexaid.org/profile or contact the IT Helpdesk for an MFA temporary bypass code.

SECTION 2: NETWORK & REMOTE ACCESS (VPN & CONNECTIVITY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 SenexAid VPN Error 809 (L2TP/IPsec Connection Failed)
- Cause: Local network firewall or ISP blocking UDP ports 500 and 4500, or Windows IPsec NAT-Traversal issues.
- Step-by-Step Resolution:
  1. Open Command Prompt (CMD) as Administrator.
  2. Run command: netsh winsock reset
  3. Registry fix for NAT-T: Ensure DWORD 'AssumeUDPEncapsulationContextOnSendRule' is set to 2 under 'HKLM\SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Services\PolicyAgent'.
  4. Reboot your computer and attempt reconnecting to 'vpn.senexaid.org'.

2.2 Bandwidth Limitation & Field Proxy Settings
- Staff in remote field locations should enable 'Low Bandwidth Mode' in enterprise tools.
- Proxy auto-configuration PAC file URL: http://proxy.senexaid.org/wpad.dat.

SECTION 3: FIELD OPERATIONS & APIN DATA INTEGRATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 APIN Data Synchronization Errors
- Issue: Field devices fail to sync local survey and support data with central APIN servers.
- Resolution Steps:
  1. Verify active internet connection (minimum 256 kbps required).
  2. Open SenexAid Sync Manager -> Settings -> Click 'Clear Cache'.
  3. Verify API endpoint is configured as: https://apin-sync.senexaid.org/v2/data.
  4. Perform a 'Force Delta Sync'. Do not interrupt power during sync.

3.2 Field Device Encryption (BitLocker)
- All laptops deployed for field research must have BitLocker enabled.
- BitLocker recovery keys are automatically backed up to Azure Active Directory (Entra ID).
- In case of a BitLocker prompt at boot, retrieve the 48-digit recovery key from https://myaccount.microsoft.com or contact IT.

SECTION 4: HARDWARE & PRINTER CONFIGURATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 Office Printer Connectivity
- Main HQ Printer IP: 192.168.10.250 (Model: HP Enterprise LaserJet)
- Installation: Navigate to Windows Settings -> Printers & Scanners -> Add Device -> Add manually via TCP/IP address '192.168.10.250'.

4.2 Workstation Performance Troubleshooting
- If experiencing system lag: Run Disk Cleanup, clear temp files ('%temp%'), and ensure at least 15% free disk space remains on C: drive.
